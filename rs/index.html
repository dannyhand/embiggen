Colour

Effect

Works like the in-game chat: start a message with colour:effect:, e.g. flash1:shake:help or purple:scroll:gf. Any hex colour works too: #ff8800:wave:hi. A prefix overrides the buttons for that sentence. In big mode, tap or swipe for the next sentence, and Esc exits.
